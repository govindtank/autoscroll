1. Overview

AutoScroll ("the App", package: com.droidtank.autoscroll) is an Android utility designed to provide hands-free automated scrolling and customized gesture touch macros. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your device.

2. Android Accessibility Service API Usage & Disclosure

AutoScroll utilizes Android's AccessibilityService API strictly and exclusively to perform the following core functions requested by the user:

  • Automated Gesture Dispatching: To mechanically dispatch simulated scroll gestures (swipe up, swipe down, swipe left, swipe right) on your screen at the velocity and easing curves configured by you.
  • Foreground Package Detection: To detect when a designated user-configured application (e.g. Chrome, eBook readers, social media apps) is opened in order to automatically trigger per-app scrolling speed profiles and display the quick-control widget.
🔒 Strict Privacy & Accessibility Guarantee:
  • The Accessibility Service NEVER reads, intercepts, monitors, or collects screen content, typed text, passwords, credit card numbers, or messages.
  • The service operates entirely locally as a blind mechanical gesture dispatcher.
  • No screen data or audio data is ever captured, stored, or transmitted.

3. Data Collection, Storage, and Sharing

  • Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or device identifiers.
  • 100% Offline & Local-First: All your custom scroll profiles, speed preferences, and gesture recordings are stored solely on your local device storage.
  • No Third-Party SDKs / No Tracking: AutoScroll contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral tracking frameworks, and no telemetry analytics libraries.
  • Zero Data Sharing: Because no data is collected, no data is ever sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

4. Required Android Permissions

  • BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE: Required to dispatch mechanical scroll gestures on behalf of the user.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: Required to display the floating translucent overlay controls over designated apps.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: (Optional) To restore automated per-app profile detection when your device reboots.

5. Children's Privacy

AutoScroll is a general utility tool and does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted from any user, our application complies fully with COPPA and Google Play Families policy requirements.
